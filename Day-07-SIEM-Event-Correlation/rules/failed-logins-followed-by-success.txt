Rule name: Repeated Failed Logins Followed by Success

Purpose:
Identify an account and source IP with at least 3 failed login
events followed by a successful login within 5 minutes.

Match conditions:
1. Same username.
2. Same source IP.
3. At least 3 failed login events.
4. A successful login occurs after the failures.
5. The successful login is within 5 minutes of the first failure.

Severity: Medium (initial triage; validate local context)

Limitations:
This is a simplified training rule. It does not establish compromise.
Production detection requires suitable event parsing, time-window
handling, deduplication and environment-specific tuning.
