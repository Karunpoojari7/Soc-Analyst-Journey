## 1. Project Overview

Day 34 focuses on transforming PAYGUARD SOC into a professional financial cybersecurity platform.

PAYGUARD is designed to monitor synthetic financial transactions, detect suspicious activity, investigate potential fraud and support defensive response workflows.

The objective is to develop a complete product experience with secure analyst authentication, real-time transaction monitoring, financial analytics and interactive SOC operations.

## 4. Detection Engineering

- DET-01: Micro-value probe followed by high-value transfer.
- DET-02: Unseen beneficiary and suspicious transaction velocity.
- DET-03: Invalid or unapproved loan disbursement.
- DET-04: Authentication abuse burst.

## 8. Development Scope

PAYGUARD operates as a controlled local financial cybersecurity lab using synthetic financial data.

It does not connect to actual banking infrastructure, UPI networks or production payment gateways. No real financial transfers are performed.
