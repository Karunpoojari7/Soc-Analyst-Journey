# Day 9 — Threat Intelligence and IOC Investigation

## Objectives
- Understand Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).
- Explore IP address reputation using VirusTotal.
- Interpret detection scores and related-indicator information.
- Understand false positives and investigation limitations.
- Preserve report integrity with SHA-256 checksums.

## Tool
VirusTotal web interface.

## Training Indicator
- Value: 192.0.2.25
- Type: IPv4 address
- Purpose: Documentation-range training example

## Key Lessons
- A detection score alone does not prove an indicator is safe or malicious.
- Related files and search results require context.
- Corroborate threat intelligence with network and endpoint evidence.
- Document observed facts separately from conclusions.
